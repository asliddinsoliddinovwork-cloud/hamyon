Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet?

No. The app works fully offline and never connects to the internet.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your phone's storage. The app never sends it anywhere, so make a backup from time to time.

What if I change phones?

Settings → Back up data. Move the file to your new phone and open it with "Restore from backup".

Where does the dollar rate come from?

You enter it yourself, for example your bank's rate. The app doesn't go online, so it doesn't update the rate automatically.

What if I forget my PIN?

The only way to reset the PIN is to erase all data. Keep your backup file so you can restore from it.

Can I unlock with Face ID or a fingerprint?

Yes. Settings → Security: turn on the PIN, then "Unlock with Face ID / Touch ID" (iPhone) or "Unlock with fingerprint" (Android). Your face or fingerprint data never reaches the app — the phone checks it itself.

Are savings added to my total balance?

No. Accounts of the "Savings" type stay apart. You move money into them with "Transfer", and it does not count as spending.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes. On your iPhone, open hamyonapp.uz/app in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen. The app runs full screen and works offline.
