Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

In short

Hamyon does not ask you to sign up and uses no ads or analytics. The financial data you enter in the app is stored only on your device.

What the app stores

Your name, accounts, income and expense records, budget, debts and settings. All of it stays in your phone's storage and is not sent to the developer or to third parties.

Internet

The Android app works offline. The iPhone web app loads its files from hamyonapp.uz and checks the same site for a new version; the data you enter is not sent.

Backups

The backup file is saved where you choose. Where to keep it and who to share it with is up to you.

PIN and biometrics

Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint checks are done by the phone itself. Face and fingerprint data is not given to the app.

Website

hamyonapp.uz is hosted on GitHub Pages. The site uses no cookies, analytics or ads; only your chosen theme (light or dark) is saved in your browser. As the hosting provider, GitHub may record a visitor's IP address in technical logs.

Deleting data

If you uninstall the app or clear the site data on iPhone, all records are deleted. Make a backup first.
